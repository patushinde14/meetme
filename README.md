
# 🌐 Prathmesh's Portfolio

Welcome to my personal portfolio website — a digital space to showcase my skills, projects, and a little bit about who I am. Whether you're a recruiter, developer, or just curious, feel free to explore!

🔗 **Live Site**: [sayhitopratham.netlify.app](https://sayhitopratham.netlify.app)

---

## ✨ Features

- Clean and responsive design
- About me section
- Skills & tech stack
- Project showcases
- Contact form (Netlify powered)
- Smooth scroll and animations

---

## 🚀 Tech Stack

- **HTML5**
- **CSS3**
- **JavaScript (Vanilla)**
- **Netlify** (for deployment and form handling)

---

## 📬 Contact Form

The contact form is powered by Netlify's built-in form handling. Messages sent from the form are processed by Netlify.

### ➕ Tip:
To receive notifications on your mobile, you can integrate Netlify Forms with **Zapier**, **Telegram Bot**, or **Pushbullet** for real-time alerts.
